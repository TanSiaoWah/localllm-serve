# Shipping Policy

**Estimated Delivery:**
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days within the contiguous United States.
- Expedited shipping: 2-3 business days for selected items.
- International shipping: 10-15 business days, depending on destination country and customs processing.
- Delivery times are calculated from the moment the order ships from the warehouse, not from the order placement date.

**Tracking:**
- Every shipment includes a tracking number sent via email once the order has been processed and dispatched.
- Tracking information is available in the customer's online account under "Order History."
- carriers used include UPS, FedEx, and USPS, depending on destination and package size.
- Real-time tracking updates are available through the carrier's website using the provided tracking number.

**Delayed Shipments:**
- If a shipment has not arrived within 5 business days of the estimated delivery date, customers should check the tracking status first, as delays may be due to carrier issues or weather.
- For orders showing no tracking updates for 7 or more business days, contact customer support for an investigation.
- If a package is lost, the customer may request a reshipment or a full refund after 14 business days from the estimated delivery date, subject to verification.
- Shipping delays due to holidays, natural disasters, or carrier strikes are not guaranteed delivery windows and may extend delivery times beyond the estimates provided.
