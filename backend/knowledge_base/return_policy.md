# Return Policy

**Return Window:** Customers may return most products within 30 days of the original delivery date.

**Eligibility Conditions:**
- The product must be in its original packaging with all included accessories, manuals, and documentation.
- The product must be in like-new condition, showing no signs of excessive use, damage, or wear.
- All original seals and protective film must remain intact where applicable.
- A valid proof of purchase (order number or receipt) must be provided.

**Refund Method:** Refunds are typically processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

**Exclusions:**
- **Opened software, digital downloads, and streaming media** cannot be returned due to licensing restrictions, unless defective.
- **Customized or personalized items** are non-refundable unless faulty.
- **Large appliances** (e.g., refrigerators, washing machines) are subject to a restocking fee of up to 15% if returned in acceptable condition.
- **Cell phones and tablets** returned more than 14 days after delivery may incur a depreciation fee based on condition.

**Can a laptop be returned after 20 days?**
Yes. A laptop can be returned within 30 days of delivery if it meets the eligibility conditions above (original packaging, like-new condition, all accessories included, and proof of purchase provided). After 30 days, the return window has closed except for defective items covered under warranty.
