# Troubleshooting Guide

**Login Issues:**
- If you are unable to log in, first verify that your username and password are correct, paying attention to capitalization and any extra spaces.
- Ensure that your Caps Lock key is off and that your keyboard layout is set to the correct language.
- If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset it via your registered email address.
- Clear your browser's cache and cookies, or try logging in using a different browser or incognito/private mode.
- If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, ensure that your authentication app or SMS code is still valid and has not expired.

**When to Escalate to Technical Support:**
- If you have reset your password and still cannot log in after two attempts.
- If your account has been locked due to multiple failed login attempts; wait 15 minutes or contact support to unlock it.
- If you are experiencing repeated authentication errors that persist across different devices and browsers, this may indicate a server-side issue.
- If you suspect unauthorized access to your account or see unfamiliar activity, contact support immediately.
- For any technical issue that prevents you from accessing core functionality after troubleshooting basic steps (e.g., the platform crashes, features are missing, or data is not loading correctly), technical support should be notified so that the issue can be investigated on the back end.

**Basic Steps Before Escalation:**
1. Refresh the page or restart the application.
2. Check your internet connection.
3. Try accessing the service from a different device.
4. Consult the status page for any known outages or maintenance.
